Key techniques

Score the thighs: rough surfaces help develop craggy crust. 60 turns: tumble during the first dry dredge to build small clumps. Tap off flour: a thick smooth layer is less desirable than visible ridges. Fry promptly: avoid leaving coated chicken to sit. Food safety: discard raw-chicken marinade and used dredge; keep raw and cooked tools separate.

Deep-fry safely: fill a heavy pot no more than one-third with oil, keep water away from hot oil, and never leave it unattended.
